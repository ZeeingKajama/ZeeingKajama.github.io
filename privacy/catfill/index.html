Privacy Policy (English)

This privacy policy applies to the All Cats mobile game application (store listing name: All Cats: Fill Puzzle, package name: com.zeeing.catfill), together with any related services operated by ZeeingStudio (collectively, the "Application"). ZeeingStudio is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require you to create an account, and the Service Provider does not operate any server that collects or stores your personal information. The Application does not contain any analytics or crash-reporting tools of the Service Provider's own, and it can be played without a network connection. However, to provide ad-supported features, the Application uses third-party services (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform and Google Play Services) that may automatically collect certain information when you use the Application. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Mobile operating system, device model, and device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID and App Set ID)
  • App usage related to ads, such as when ads are shown and how you interact with them
  • General diagnostic data related to ads and app stability
Data Stored Only on Your Device (Progress & Settings):
The Application stores the following directly on your device: your settings (sound effects, music, vibration and language), the stars and best move count for each level you have cleared, the last level you played, an unfinished level so that you can resume it, the number of hint tokens you hold, and counters used to space out ads (levels cleared and time elapsed since the last full-screen ad). This data is never transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the Service Provider. It is not included in Android cloud backups, so it is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Application or clear its data. You can also erase your level progress at any time with [Reset progress] in Settings; your settings and hint tokens are kept.
Device Permissions:
The Application does not request permission to access your photos, files, contacts, location, camera or microphone, and it does not read your device's motion sensors. The Application uses these standard permissions: internet and network state (for ads), vibration (for in-game haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings) and the advertising ID (used by Google AdMob). The Google libraries in the Application (AdMob and Play services) also declare standard permissions they need to run, such as keeping background tasks running; Android grants these automatically.
Advertisements & Rewarded Ads:
To keep the Application free, ads are provided by Google AdMob. Full-screen (interstitial) ads may appear between levels, after a level has been cleared, and never while you are playing a level. You may choose to watch a rewarded video ad to receive a hint token; rewarded ads are shown only after you tap a button that says so. If you decline an ad or no ad is available, you can still keep playing. Reward eligibility and timing are processed locally on your device.

Consent, Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may use identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, limit ad frequency and measure ad performance. Where required by applicable law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time with [Privacy options] in Settings. Whatever you choose, you can play the whole game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party services listed below, to the extent needed to provide the features described above. Their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • GitHub Pages (hosts this policy)

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Because the Service Provider holds no personal data on its own servers:

  • Local progress can be deleted at any time with [Reset progress] in Settings. Uninstalling the Application or clearing its data deletes everything it has stored, including settings and hint tokens.
  • Ad data can be controlled through [Privacy options] and your device's ad settings described above, or from your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).

For any other request or question, contact the Service Provider at bbdbarbd@gmail.com.

Data Retention

  • Local progress and settings: Kept on your device until you reset them, uninstall the Application or clear its data.
  • Ad and diagnostic data: Kept by Google AdMob in accordance with Google's policies.
  • Access logs for this page: Kept by GitHub in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at bbdbarbd@gmail.com so that we can take the necessary action.

Security

Progress and settings on your device are kept in the Application's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Communication with Google services is encrypted by those services. If a security incident affecting your personal information occurs, the Service Provider will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-25.

Android 클라우드 백업에도 포함되지 않으므로, 애플리케이션을 삭제하거나 앱 데이터를 지우면 영구히 삭제됩니다. 설정의 [진행 초기화]로 레벨 진행 기록을 언제든 지울 수도 있으며, 이때 설정과 힌트 토큰은 남습니다.
기기 권한:
본 애플리케이션은 사진·파일·연락처·위치·카메라·마이크 접근 권한을 요청하지 않으며, 기기의 동작 센서도 읽지 않습니다. 애플리케이션이 사용하는 일반 권한은 다음과 같습니다: 인터넷 및 네트워크 상태(광고), 진동(게임 중 진동 피드백 — 설정에서 끌 수 있음), 광고 ID(Google AdMob이 사용). 애플리케이션에 포함된 Google 라이브러리(AdMob, Play 서비스)도 동작에 필요한 일반 권한(예: 백그라운드 작업 유지)을 선언하며, 이 권한들은 Android가 자동으로 허용합니다.
