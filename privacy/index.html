Privacy Policy (English)

This privacy policy applies to the Brain Combo mobile game application (package name: com.zeeing.braingame), together with any related services operated by ZeeingStudio (collectively, the "Application"). ZeeingStudio is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require you to create an account, and the Service Provider does not operate any server that collects or stores your personal information. However, to provide leaderboards, achievements and ad-supported features, the Application uses third-party services (Google Play Services, Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob) that may automatically collect certain information when you use the Application. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Mobile operating system, device model, and advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • Scores submitted to leaderboards and achievement progress
  • Ad interaction data and general diagnostic data related to ads and app stability
Data Stored Only on Your Device (Records & Settings):
The Application stores your settings (sound, vibration and language), scores and personal bests, play history, streaks, monthly stamps and badges, Brain Animal test results, and daily test tickets directly on your device. This data is never transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the Service Provider. It is not included in Android cloud backups, so it is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Application or clear its data.
Device Permissions:
The Application does not request access to your photos, files, contacts, location, camera or microphone. It uses only the following standard permissions: internet and network state (for leaderboards, achievements and ads), vibration (for in-game haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings), and the advertising ID (used by Google AdMob).
Sharing Results & Friend Challenge Codes:
When you tap [Brag] on a results screen, the Application creates a score card image in its own private storage and opens the Android share sheet. The image and text (score, game name, challenge code and store link) are sent only to the app or person you choose. A challenge code (e.g., BM-XXXX-XXXX) contains only the game and the score — no personal information. When you tap [Paste] on the Ranks screen, the Application reads your clipboard once to get the challenge code; it does not read the clipboard at any other time, and received challenges are not saved.
Leaderboards & Achievements (Google Play Games Services):
If you sign in to Google Play Games, the Application connects to Google Play Games Services (PGS). Through PGS, your player ID, display name, avatar, submitted scores and achievement progress may be processed by Google to show rankings and unlock achievements. Signing in is optional, and you can play every game without it.
Advertisements & Rewarded Ads:
To keep the Application free, ads are provided by Google AdMob. Interstitial ads may appear between screens (never during a game), and you may choose to watch rewarded video ads to take an extra Brain Animal test or restore a broken streak. Reward eligibility and timing are processed locally on your device.

Consent, Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may use identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, limit ad frequency and measure ad performance. Where required by applicable law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice from the consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party services listed below, to the extent needed to provide the features described above. Their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games
  • Google AdMob
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Because the Service Provider holds no personal data on its own servers:

  • Local records can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the Application or clearing its data.
  • Google Play Games data (profile, leaderboard scores, achievements) can be managed or deleted from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).
  • Ad data can be controlled through the consent form and your device's ad settings described above.

For any other request or question, contact the Service Provider at bbdbarbd@gmail.com.

Data Retention

  • Local records and settings: Kept on your device until you uninstall the Application or clear its data.
  • Leaderboard & achievement data: Kept by Google Play Games Services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Ad and diagnostic data: Kept by Google AdMob in accordance with Google's policies.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at bbdbarbd@gmail.com so that we can take the necessary action.

Security

Records on your device are kept in the Application's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Communication with Google services is encrypted by those services. If a security incident affecting your personal information occurs, the Service Provider will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-10-19.
